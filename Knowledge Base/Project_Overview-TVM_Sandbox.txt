SUMMARY
-------
This is the CRM for an automotive dealership digital-marketing agency. It
covers dealer accounts, OEM co-op programs, media budgets, creative and
compliance requests, onboarding, and a large support-case operation.

There are two business lines:
  - TVM (Team Velocity)
  - Foundation / FNDN (Foundation Digital)
The two lines share the org through separate record types and "Hybrid"
billing record types.

8. THINGS WORTH A LOOK
----------------------
1. Several triggers on the same object (two on Case, two on
   ContentDocumentLink, three on OpportunityLineItem) plus dozens of flows
   on the same objects. There is no guaranteed order between them, which
   risks recursion and governor-limit errors.

2. 100 inactive autolaunched flows and 3 inactive triggers. These are
   candidates for clean-up.

3. Risky-looking permission sets: "Unrequire MFA", "Disable MFA",
   "Hard Delete", "View all data", "Query All", and test-like names "abc",
   "abc1" and "abcs". These deserve a security review.

4. Foundation vs TVM logic is duplicated (_FNDN triggers, PDFs and case
   flows alongside the TVM versions). This fits the repo's FD Foundation
   Record requirement and is worth mapping before adding more.
